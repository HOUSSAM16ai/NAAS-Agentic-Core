# Foreign-Currency Operating System — Exportable Value from Algeria

## Purpose

This document is the commercial counterpart to the engineering constitution. It does not promise revenue; it defines the evidence-driven operating system that can produce revenue without confusing research, prototypes, attention, or unpaid usage with money received.

## Value chain

```text
Research and scarce capability
        ↓
Verified engineering asset
        ↓
Customer problem and willingness-to-pay evidence
        ↓
Narrow exportable offer
        ↓
Paid pilot with bounded acceptance criteria
        ↓
Repeatable delivery, support, and renewal
        ↓
Foreign-currency revenue, margin, skills, and Algerian jobs
```

## Offer readiness contract

| State | Minimum evidence | What an agent may say |
|---|---|---|
| `PROPOSED` | Technical hypothesis and target buyer defined. | “We are exploring this offer.” |
| `DISCOVERY` | Interviews or primary market evidence recorded; no sales claim. | “The problem is being investigated.” |
| `OFFER_READY` | Scope, pricing hypothesis, delivery owner, acceptance criteria, and risk boundary. | “The offer is packaged for a controlled pilot.” |
| `PILOT` | Pilot scope, buyer approval, success metric, and delivery plan. | “A bounded pilot is running.” |
| `PAID_PROOF` | Contract, invoice, order, or payment evidence; technical scope remains bounded. | “Paid proof exists within the documented scope.” |
| `REPEATABLE` | Repeated paid delivery or renewal, margin, support process, and risk review. | “The offer has repeatable evidence within the documented market.” |

## Commercial acceptance packet

Every offer advancement includes the following fields:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Named segment and decision-maker, not “the market”. |
| Paid problem | Cost, risk, delay, or missed value the buyer already recognizes. |
| Offer boundary | What is delivered, what is excluded, and the acceptance test. |
| Currency route | Lawful invoicing and collection route, with professional review where required. |
| Evidence | Primary interviews, pilot results, contract/payment, renewal, and source dates. |
| Delivery economics | Owner, estimated effort, direct cost, support burden, and margin hypothesis. |
| Risk | Security, data, legal, model, dependency, and concentration risks. |
| Algerian value | Skills, local cost base, jobs, supplier development, or export capability, measured without inflated claims. |

## Stop conditions

An agent must stop and request human/business review when the buyer, legal scope, data rights, payment route, safety impact, or delivery economics are unknown. It must not fabricate a price, contract, market size, customer quote, exchange-rate result, employment effect, or regulatory conclusion.

A technically impressive feature that cannot identify a paid problem enters the research backlog. A commercially attractive promise without reproducible technical evidence remains a discovery hypothesis. This separation protects the product from both engineering theatre and sales theatre.
