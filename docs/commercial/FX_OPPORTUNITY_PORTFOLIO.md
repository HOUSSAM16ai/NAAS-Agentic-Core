# Future Foreign-Currency Opportunity Portfolio

> **Current state (2026-09-01): research only.** This portfolio ranks future export offers; it records no customer, contract, payment, recurring revenue, product-market fit, or guaranteed return. Canonical machine-readable data lives in [`FX_OPPORTUNITY_PORTFOLIO.json`](FX_OPPORTUNITY_PORTFOLIO.json).

## 1. Decision boundary

The portfolio explores offer variants **inside** the seven revenue lines authorized by [D-273](../DEEP_TECH_CONSTITUTION.md). It does not create an eighth line, advance an offer in [`OFFER_CATALOG.json`](OFFER_CATALOG.json), or authorize external contact. Each variant must start as a bounded service experiment and earn product investment through paid evidence.

This applies D-273 laws **L1–L8**: scarce technical depth; an Arabic/French or private-deployment wedge; an external institutional buyer; and documented competition before build. It also applies the stop conditions in the [`FOREIGN_CURRENCY_OPERATING_SYSTEM`](FOREIGN_CURRENCY_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md).

## 3. Evidence that changes the plan

- **NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative:** agent identity, authorization, protocol security, and security evaluation are active standardization/research needs. This supports a problem direction, not a NAAS budget.
- **MCP Security Best Practices and RFC 9700:** confused-deputy, token-passthrough, SSRF, session, consent, audience, and scope failures create a concrete assessment surface.
- **AgentDojo and InjecAgent:** tool-using agents can be evaluated jointly for utility and security under indirect prompt injection; benchmark rates must not be projected onto a customer.
- **AraSafe, AraTrust, BALSAM, and HELM Arabic:** Arabic safety and trustworthiness evaluation is active and increasingly supplied. This supports specialization **and** weakens any claim that the niche is empty.
- **EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, and NIST TEVV work:** traceability, documentation, risk controls, human oversight, robustness, and structured evaluation create evidence-engineering work. The Commission's Service Desk and incumbent GRC/security platforms create strong substitution risk.
- **Microsoft Entra/Purview/Defender:** major platforms already bundle agent identity, governance, data security, and runtime protection. NAAS must win through independent cross-runtime evidence, Arabic specialization, or service depth—not a generic control checkbox.

## 4. Operating sequence

1. Sell learning before software: problem interview → redacted trace or evidence sample → bounded paid sprint.
2. Build only what repeats across unrelated paid customers; record delivery hours, direct cost, support burden, and customer-measured baseline/outcome.
3. Promote a variant only through the canonical readiness ladder. An external paper, regulation, acquisition, or benchmark never promotes commercial status.
4. Review the ranking quarterly and after every paid experiment. Apply each kill criterion without preserving a favored narrative.
5. Keep long-horizon industrial and formal-verification work as research until a buyer, partner, data boundary, acceptance test, and lawful currency route exist.

### Decision-ready discovery for the two NOW opportunities

The two near-term opportunities now include a machine-checked `discovery_protocol` in the [canonical portfolio](FX_OPPORTUNITY_PORTFOLIO.json). It turns a generic request to “talk to customers” into an evidence boundary: a qualified buyer definition, explicit contact authorization, at least five problem questions, a minimal de-identified evidence record, a bounded paid-pilot boundary, and an explicit continue/kill decision rule.

The protocol is intentionally not a sales claim. A recorded interview is neither qualified demand nor revenue; only the published paid-pilot gate can create the next experiment. The record excludes names, secrets, access tokens, customer data, and unapproved third-party testing. `NOW` means discovery may proceed after authorization; `OPTION` and `WATCH` remain research items and cannot acquire a discovery protocol until promoted by evidence.
